# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People evaluating Hugo Martins as a product builder, including peers, collaborators, hiring teams, and visitors interested in his work. They need a quick, credible view of his experience, projects, thinking, and personality.

## Product Purpose

Hugo's personal site presents his professional record and selected product work in a direct, memorable way. It should make the quality and range of his work legible without availability signalling or marketing filler.

## Brand Personality

Precise, opinionated, playful.

## Anti-references

- Generic portfolio templates and polished SaaS landing pages.
- Inspirational filler, self-explaining copy, and availability signalling.
- Decorative interactions that obscure content or slow navigation.
- Editorial affectations added without a clear purpose.

## Design Principles

1. **State the fact and stop.** Claims and copy should be specific enough to stand without disclaimers.
2. **Let the work carry the page.** Projects, outcomes, and real details matter more than ornamental framing.
3. **Use personality with intent.** Distinctive moments should reveal something about Hugo or the work.
4. **Keep navigation immediate.** Visitors should reach any shipped surface quickly at every viewport size.
5. **Preserve the established system.** New elements can break character locally when the content justifies it, but should remain coherent with the site's typography, spacing, and theme behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard navigation, clear focus states, reduced-motion support, semantic landmarks, and touch targets of at least 44 pixels where practical.
